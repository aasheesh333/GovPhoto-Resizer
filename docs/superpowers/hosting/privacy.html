Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GovPhoto Resizer ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our Android application. The app is published by Dhanuk Softwares ("operator") from India. By using the app, you agree to the terms below.

1. What we do NOT collect

  • Photos: Every photo you select or capture is processed entirely on your device. We never upload your photo or signature to any server.
  • Background removal & face detection: Run using on-device ML Kit models. No image data leaves your device.
  • Photo history: Stored only in your device's local app data. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app erases all of it.

2. What we do collect

  • Anonymous analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to count feature usage, crashes, and app-launch events. This data is anonymized and aggregated; it is never tied to your name, email, or photo content.
  • Crash diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics records stack traces when the app crashes. These may include device model, OS version, and a random installation UUID — but never your photo, signature, or any government identifier.
  • Push notification tokens: OneSignal stores a push token (an opaque string) so we can send you exam-deadline or release announcements, if you opt in. We do not link this token to your identity.

3. Advertising

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use your advertising ID and approximate device characteristics to serve relevant ads, subject to Google's own privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalization in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Subscribing to GovPhoto Pro removes all advertisements and disables the ads SDK entirely for your session.

4. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your card or UPI details. RevenueCat is our subscription-management partner and receives your Google Play account ID to verify entitlement. RevenueCat does not receive your photo content.

5. Permissions the app requests

  • Camera: To capture a fresh photo inside the app for resizing.
  • Storage / Media access (Android 13+): To read photos from your gallery.
  • Internet: For loading ads (free users only), Firebase analytics/crash report upload, push notifications, and the link you tapped to open this page.
  • Post notifications (Android 13+): To receive push notifications, if you opt in.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general use, including minors preparing for government exams. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Exam-deadline notifications are opt-in only.

7. Data retention

  • Device-stored history: retained until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Firebase analytics: retained per Google's default (currently ~14 months).
  • Crash reports: retained for ~90 days in Crashlytics.
  • OneSignal tokens: removed automatically when you uninstall, or on request to support.

8. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023, India)

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you (limited — most of our data is anonymous aggregates).
  • Request correction or erasure of any personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics or push notifications at any time — disable in Settings → Notifications, or unsubscribe from emails.
  • Lodge a grievance with support (see Contact page).

9. International transfers

Firebase, RevenueCat and Google AdMob are operated from outside India and may process data in their global infrastructure. Each is GDPR/CCPA/DPDP-aware and offers transfers under Standard Contractual Clauses. By using the app you consent to such transfers for the limited data described above.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when material changes are made. The "Last updated" date above will be revised.
